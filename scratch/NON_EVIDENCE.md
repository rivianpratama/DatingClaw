# Scratch outputs are non-evidence

The scripts and `.npy`/`.npz` files in this directory are exploratory. They do
not carry sample IDs, manifests, recipe hashes, calibration hashes, or policy
provenance, so they must not be cited as measured results or used to promote a
model.

Use `bumbleclaw_v6.experiments.*` and the immutable run artifacts under
`D:/bumbleclaw_v6r1/results/` for evidence. If a scratch result matters, port
the method into a versioned experiment module and rerun it under the matched
protocol.
